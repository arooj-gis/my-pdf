    About the institute

    Institute of Geo-Information & Earth Observation

    The Institute of Geo-Information and Earth Observation (IGEO) is an academic and research institution dedicated to the science and application of geospatial technologies. It integrates remote sensing, geographic information systems and spatial data science to address problems in agriculture, environment, urban growth and natural-resource management.

    Through rigorous coursework, field-based research and collaboration with industry and government, the institute prepares graduates to turn Earth observation data into reliable decisions for sustainable development.

    • Remote sensing and image analysis
    • GIS, WebGIS and spatial databases
    • Climate, land-use and crop monitoring
    • Applied research and professional training

    Lab questions

    Answers for your submission

    1. Purpose of CSS gradients

    Gradients blend two or more colors smoothly, giving the background depth and a modern look without image files. Here they create the light blue to white theme, separate the page from the content cards and keep it fast to load.

    2. Making items clickable

    The <button> element with an onclick attribute makes the contact items clickable and keyboard accessible. For real links, the <a> element with href is used, such as href="mailto:name@example.com".

    3. Why showPopup and closePopup

    JavaScript responds to user actions. showPopup() fills the modal with the right content and makes it visible; closePopup() hides it again. This makes the page interactive without reloading and lets one modal serve many buttons.

    4. Three-column layout

    It groups related content into clear zones: contact on the left, profile in the center and institute on the right. This creates visual balance, a natural reading flow, a clear hierarchy and an easy responsive stack on small screens.

    5. Benefit of modal popups

    Modals keep the main page clean and focused by showing secondary details only when needed. They save space, draw attention to one piece of information at a time and avoid navigating to another page.
